Common Questions

Answers for your peace of mind

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

How long does a website or system take to build?
A standard business website takes 1–2 weeks. A custom management system (like the Food Store database) typically takes 2–4 weeks depending on the complexity. I give you a clear timeline before we start, and I update you weekly.
What happens if the system breaks after launch?
I don't just build and disappear. All projects come with 30 days of free post-launch support to fix any bugs. After that, you can hire me for a monthly IT support retainer to keep things running smoothly.
How do payments work?
To secure your spot in my schedule, I require a 50% deposit upfront. The remaining 50% is paid only when you are 100% happy with the final product and ready to go live.
Do you offer training for my staff?
Yes. For custom systems (like the school food store), I provide a 1-hour training session for your team and a written guide so they know exactly how to use it.
